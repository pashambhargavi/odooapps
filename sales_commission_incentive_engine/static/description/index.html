COMPLETE VISUAL PRODUCT TOUR

Step-by-Step Screen Walkthrough

Every screen below ships ready to use out of the box with sales_commission_incentive_engine.

SCREEN 1 OF 6

1. Commission Plans Kanban Dashboard

Go to Commissions → Configuration → Commission Plans. View and manage all commercial incentive models (Standard Revenue, Margin Protection, Tiered Accelerators, Affiliate Partner Fees) with live counts of active sales reps and accrued lines.

SCREEN 2 OF 6

2. Dynamic Incentive Configuration & Volume Tiers

Easily configure progressive volume thresholds (e.g. 0-$10k @ 3%, $10k-$50k @ 5%, >$50k @ 8%), trigger events (Invoice Paid, Invoice Posted, SO Confirmation), and sales manager override percentages.

SCREEN 3 OF 6

3. Real-Time Commission Calculation on Sales Orders

On every Sales Order and Quotation, the module computes real-time Salesperson Commission, Manager Override, and Affiliate Partner payouts with a dedicated smart button and itemized line breakdown.

SCREEN 4 OF 6

4. Centralized Commission Ledger & Pivot Analysis

Go to Commissions → Commission Ledger. View every earned incentive line across orders and invoices with multi-dimensional filtering by salesperson, applied plan, role, and calendar month.

SCREEN 5 OF 6

5. Settlement Worksheets & 1-Click Vendor Bill Generation

Go to Commissions → Settlement Worksheets. Click Calculate Commissions to aggregate lines for the chosen month. Upon manager approval, click Create Settlement Bill to generate an accounting Vendor Bill for external contractors!

SCREEN 6 OF 6

6. Printable PDF Commission Settlement Statement

Generate clean, auditor-ready PDF Commission Statements detailing sales volume, itemized orders and invoices, commission rates, net payable totals, and dual manager/rep signature blocks.
