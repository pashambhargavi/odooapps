ENTERPRISE DEPLOYMENT PLAYBOOK

7-Step Verification & Feature Walkthrough

STEP 1 One-Click Module Installation

Download and extract omni_theme_multi_studio into your custom addons directory. Update App List and install.

STEP 2 Inspect Executive 10-Theme Login Page

Log out to view the high-impact split-screen login page showcasing the active company theme and the 10-theme roster preview badge.

STEP 3 Trigger 1-Click Systray Switcher

Click the OmniTheme toggle icon in the top-right navbar. The 10-theme dropdown opens with color swatches and instant click handlers.

STEP 4 Test Instant Reactive Zero-Reload Switch

Select Cyberpunk Neon, Obsidian Luxe, or Pristine Paper. Observe the entire viewport adapt dynamically in <50ms without reloading your active document.

STEP 5 Personal User Preference Persistence

Navigate to your user profile preferences. Confirm your chosen theme is persisted, ensuring consistent aesthetics across subsequent logins and devices.

STEP 6 Configure Company-Wide Governance

Navigate to Settings > OmniTheme 10-in-1 Studio to enforce company defaults, customize button corner radius (8px / 20px / 4px), or lock switching.

STEP 7 Automated Unit Test Verification

Execute odoo-bin -i omni_theme_multi_studio --test-enable. Confirm all 5 test cases pass with 0 errors across your target database.
