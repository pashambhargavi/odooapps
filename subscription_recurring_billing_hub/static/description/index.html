HOW IT WORKS • 6 PROVEN STEPS

Explore the Application in Action

STEP 1

Visual Subscription Kanban Dashboard

Manage all customer subscriptions grouped by lifecycle stages: Draft, Active, Paused, Expired, and Closed. Every card highlights the contract reference, client, next billing date, and real-time MRR/ARR badges.

  • Instant visibility over recurring revenue run-rate
  • Color-coded status alerts for expired or paused accounts
  • Direct 1-click access to contract details and invoice ledger
STEP 2

Comprehensive Contract & Invoicing Hub

The contract form manages itemized service lines, tax calculations, discounts, next invoice scheduling, and normalized recurring revenue metrics.

  • Statusbar actions: Activate, Pause, Resume, and Renew
  • 1-Click "Generate Recurring Invoice" for immediate on-demand billing
  • Smart stat button linking all generated customer invoices
STEP 3

Versatile Multi-Cycle Billing Plans

Configure recurring plans tailored to your business model. Supports Monthly Retainers, Quarterly Maintenance Agreements, and Annual Support Licences.

  • Toggle automated daily invoice creation via cron scheduler
  • Configure optional auto-posting for zero-touch accounting
  • Set advance expiration reminder windows (e.g. 7, 14, 30 days)
STEP 4

Convert Confirmed Sales Orders in 1 Click

Empower your sales reps to close subscription deals using familiar Odoo quotations. Once confirmed, clicking "Create Subscription" generates and activates the recurring contract instantly.

  • Copies order lines, products, quantities, prices, and discounts
  • Creates bidirectional link between quotation and subscription
  • Eliminates human error and manual contract re-entry
STEP 5

1-Click Renewal & Price Adjustment Wizard

Never lose a renewing client. The intuitive renewal wizard allows account managers to extend contracts by recurring cycles or set custom dates, with optional annual price indexation.

  • Extend by N cycles (e.g. +12 months for 1 year renewal)
  • Apply automatic price increases (e.g. +5% inflation adjustment)
  • Reactivates expired accounts and updates next billing dates
STEP 6

Professional PDF Subscription Service Agreement

Generate and print formal, customer-ready Subscription Service Agreements with itemized recurring products, normalized MRR totals, start/expiration dates, and dual signature blocks.

  • Itemized products, service descriptions, unit rates, and discounts
  • Highlighted Monthly (MRR) and Annual (ARR) contract value
  • Company authorization and customer signature confirmation blocks
