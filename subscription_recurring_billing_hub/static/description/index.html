Subscription, Recurring Billing & Contract Management Hub

Standard Odoo Community Edition provides zero recurring billing functionality. Stop generating invoices by hand every week, month, or year. This robust enterprise module automates your entire subscription lifecycle: recurring billing cycles, automated invoicing crons, MRR & ARR run-rate analytics, and 1-click renewal wizards.

Key Highlights

Core enterprise capabilities engineered for high-volume recurring billing workflows.

✓

Multi-Frequency Billing Plans

Configure flexible billing cycles: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually with configurable advance alert windows (7, 14, 30 days).

✓

Zero-Touch Automated Invoicing

Built-in background cron scheduler runs daily, identifies due contracts, generates customer invoices, steps forward the next billing date, and posts audit notes.

✓

Real-Time MRR & ARR Analytics

Automatic calculation of Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) and Annual Run-Rate (ARR) normalized across all contract billing frequencies.

✓

1-Click Sales Order Conversion

Quote subscriptions on standard sales orders. One click on "Create Subscription" converts products, quantities, prices, and discounts instantly.

✓

1-Click Renewal Extension Wizard

Seamlessly extend expiring contracts by custom cycles or set end dates with optional percentage price indexation for annual uplifts.

✓

Printable Subscription Agreement (PDF)

Generate formal, legal PDF contract agreements complete with recurring terms, line item breakdowns, MRR summaries, and signature blocks.
