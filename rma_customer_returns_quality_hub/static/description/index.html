RMA, Customer Returns & Quality Inspection Hub

Stop handling product returns through scattered emails and disconnected reverse pickings. This complete hub delivers end-to-end Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA), receiving dock operations, quality inspection workbenches, and 1-click financial reconciliations directly in Odoo Community.

Key Highlights

Core enterprise capabilities engineered for high-volume customer return workflows.

✓

Complete 5-Stage Lifecycle

Full workflow control: Draft → Approved → Goods Received → Inspected → Resolved with complete chatter audit trails.

✓

1-Click Ticket Generation

Generate pre-filled RMA requests straight from confirmed Sales Orders, validated Deliveries, or Customer Invoices with zero re-entry.

✓

Reverse Stock Receiving Dock

Automatically creates incoming warehouse pickings from the customer location into the target warehouse dock upon RMA approval.

✓

Quality Inspection Workbench

Inspect incoming items individually: record accepted units for restock, mark defective units for scrap, or route items for internal repair.

✓

Automated Credit Notes & Refunds

Instantly posts customer credit notes (`out_refund`) linked directly to the RMA ticket with accurate pricing, taxes, and financial accounts.

✓

Printable Customer Return Slip

Official PDF return authorization slip with RMA barcode, return package instructions, authorized quantities, and shipping address.

Screenshots & Detailed Walkthrough

See how the module operates across real-world business scenarios.

STEP 1

Centralized RMA Order Management

Manage return tickets across all status stages with full breakdown of returned units, refund totals, and linked orders.

STEP 2

1-Click Ticket Creation from Sales & Invoices

Support teams initiate returns directly from Sales Orders or Invoices. Lines and unit prices populate automatically.

STEP 3

Automated Reverse Stock Transfer Receipt

Receiving warehouse operations are generated automatically, ensuring real-time stock location tracking upon arrival.

STEP 4

Quality Inspection Workbench

Warehouse technicians record inspected quantities, test observations, and select the final disposition (Refund, Replace, Scrap, Repair).

STEP 5

Automated Customer Refund Credit Note

Generates balanced accounting credit notes directly from the RMA ticket, eliminating discrepancies between inventory and finance.

STEP 6

Kanban Returns Pipeline & Visibility

Visual drag-and-drop kanban board gives operations managers immediate insight into pending receipts, inspections, and resolutions.
