Quality Assurance & Live Testing Guide

7-Step End-to-End Testing & Verification Playbook

Follow this exact step-by-step workflow to verify shift generation, automatic conflict prevention, bidding, peer swaps, and PDF reporting in your local database.

STEP 1 Shift Template Configuration & Net Hour Math
Action: Navigate to Shift Roster → Configuration → Shift Templates. Inspect pre-loaded templates (Morning 07:00-15:30 with 0.5h break, Night 23:00-07:30 with +25% differential).
Verification: Confirm net duration calculates automatically as 8.0 hours.
STEP 2 Generate Master Duty Roster Slots
Action: Go to Duty Rosters → Create. Enter Department ("Emergency Medicine"), Start Date (Next Monday), End Date (Next Sunday). Select Morning, Evening, and Night templates. Click Generate Shift Slots.
Verification: System populates individual shift records for all 7 days with calculated start and end datetimes.
STEP 3 Run One-Click Auto-Scheduler
Action: On the Duty Roster form, click the ⚡ Auto-Schedule button.
Verification: Employees from the target department are assigned to unfilled slots across the week respecting maximum weekly limits.
STEP 4 Overlap Conflict Shield Validation
Action: Deliberately assign the same employee to two overlapping shift slots on the same date and click Check Conflicts.
Verification: The system flags both shifts with red warning alerts (has_conflict=True) and logs the exact overlapping schedule reference in the conflict notes.
STEP 5 Open Shift Bidding & Manager Award
Action: Open any unassigned shift, set State to Open for Bidding. Click Bid on Shift with an eligible employee, then click Award to Selected Employee.
Verification: Shift status transitions to Confirmed with the chosen employee assigned.
STEP 6 Peer Shift Swap & Exchange Workflow
Action: Navigate to Shift Swaps → Create. Select Shift A (Nurse Alex Morgan) and Shift B (Nurse Priya Patel). Click Submit for Approval, then Approve Trade.
Verification: Employees on both shifts are swapped instantly with swap history logged in both shift records.
STEP 7 Printable Duty Roster Matrix PDF
Action: On the Duty Roster form, click Print → Weekly Duty Roster Matrix.
Verification: High-definition A4 PDF generates with daily employee shifts, hours subtotal, overtime notes, and supervisor signature blocks.
