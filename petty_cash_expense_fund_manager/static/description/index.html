Petty Cash, Multi-Custodian Imprest Fund & Expense Manager

Stop managing office cash in loose envelopes and uncontrolled spreadsheets. This comprehensive accounting module delivers complete Multi-Custodian Cash Boxes, Imprest Float Cycles, Physical Denomination Tally Sheets, and 1-Click Bank Replenishment Entries directly in Odoo Community.

Key Highlights

Core enterprise capabilities engineered for tight internal control and frictionless cash expense reconciliation.

✓

Multi-Custodian Cash Floats

Set up isolated petty cash registers across branches, departments, or project sites with designated custodians, fixed imprest float limits, and GL asset accounts.

✓

Imprest Replenishment Cycle

1-click replenishment requests group all approved vouchers, generate bank transfer journal entries, and automatically restore cash float capacity to 100%.

✓

Denomination Tally Counter

Count physical cash bills ($100, $50, $20, $10, $5, $1) and coins. The workbench instantly calculates cash overage or shortage discrepancies against expected GL balances.

✓

Instant GL Accounting Entry

Paying a voucher automatically creates and posts a balanced journal entry in Odoo General Ledger: debits expense accounts and credits the petty cash asset account.

✓

Printable 3-Signature Slip (PDF)

Formal printable PDF payment voucher slips complete with line item breakdowns, expense categorization, and signature blocks for Payee, Custodian, and Finance.

✓

Replenishment Threshold Alerts

Automated threshold monitoring alerts custodians and finance controllers when a cash box drops below minimum operating float, preventing cash runouts.

Screenshots & Detailed Walkthrough

STEP 1

Multi-Custodian Cash Boxes & Real-Time Balances

Visual Kanban dashboard displaying active cash floats, accountable custodians, current cash in box, and dynamic replenishment threshold alert badges.

STEP 2

Petty Cash Expense Slip & Instant Journal Entry

Fast voucher creation with expense accounts, cost centers, payee details, and 1-click cash disbursement posting to general ledger.

STEP 3

Physical Cash Count & Denomination Tally Sheet

Physical register count workbench ($100, $50, $20, $10, $5, $1 bills & coins). Instantly highlights cash over/short discrepancies.

STEP 4

Imprest Fund Bank Replenishment Order

Group paid vouchers into a formal reimbursement request. Finance approval triggers a bank transfer entry, restoring the cash float to 100%.

STEP 5

Formal Printable Expense Voucher (PDF)

Printable disbursement slips with itemized expense lines, GL accounting distribution, and 3 signature blocks for Payee, Custodian, and Finance.

STEP 6

Cash Count Audit History & Discrepancy Tracking

Complete audit history tracking all denomination counts, date/time timestamps, expected vs actual cash totals, and discrepancy badges.
