Customer Credit, Overdue Aging & Margin Risk Guard

Stop bad debt and unprofitable orders before confirmation. Enforce Customer Credit Limits, Overdue Invoice Aging Gates, Minimum Profit Margin %, and One-Click Finance Override Approvals in Odoo 19 Community.

4-Point Automated Commercial Risk Protection

1. Total Receivable & Uninvoiced Order Exposure

Computes live customer exposure across Unpaid Posted Invoices + Uninvoiced Confirmed Sales Orders + Current Quotation Total and compares against Policy or Custom Customer Credit Limits.

2. Overdue Invoice Aging Gate

Even if a customer is below their total credit limit, automatically hold new orders when they have unpaid invoices overdue beyond the allowed aging days (e.g., 30 days) or overdue invoice count.

3. Real-Time Sales Line & Order Profit Margin %

Brings Unit Cost, Margin Amount, and Margin % directly onto Sales Order lines and totals in Odoo 19 Community—and blocks orders falling below your minimum profit margin %.

4. Structured Finance Override & Audit Trail

When an order is held, an auditable Credit & Margin Override Request (CRO/2026/0001) is created. Credit Controllers can approve & auto-confirm the order or reject with mandatory feedback.
