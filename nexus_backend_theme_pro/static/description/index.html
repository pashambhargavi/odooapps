QUALITY ASSURANCE PLAYBOOK

7-Step Verification & Testing Runbook

Follow this comprehensive sequence to verify all features in under 3 minutes.

Step 1: Install Addon & Observe Split-Screen Login

Install nexus_backend_theme_pro. Log out or open a private window to verify the corporate branded split-screen login layout with hero stats and credentials form.

Step 2: Inspect SaaS Collapsible Left Sidebar

Log into the backend. Observe the modern SaaS left sidebar with company modules, active state indicators, and fast keyword search.

Step 3: Cycle 3-in-1 Navigation Layouts via Systray

Click the layout switcher icon in the top-right systray. Observe the UI dynamically transition from Sidebar to Enterprise Topbar, and to Slim Mini-Rail with zero page reload.

Step 4: Toggle Smart Side-by-Side Chatter

Open any Sales Order or CRM form view. Click the columns icon in the systray to toggle chatter from bottom stacked to side-by-side right column with independent scrolling.

Step 5: Verify Compact Financial Data Density & Sticky Headers

Navigate to Accounting Journal Items or Invoices list view. Notice the compact 4px row padding, high-density visibility, and sticky column headers during deep scrolling.

Step 6: Test Mobile Viewport & Curved Floating Dock

Switch browser to mobile simulation (iPhone/Android). Verify the curved floating dock pinned to the bottom of the viewport with responsive touch shortcuts.

Step 7: Configure Corporate Palettes in Nexus Studio

Navigate to Settings > Nexus Theme Studio. Switch between Royal Sapphire, FinTech Emerald, Amethyst, and Dark presets to rebrand the instance instantly.
