THE WORKFLOW AT A GLANCE

How the Approval Flow Works

Every document follows a clear 5-stage lifecycle, from rule definition to automatic confirmation.

STEP 1 • Define Rule

Set amount or discount thresholds and assign Tier 1 to Tier 3 approvers.

STEP 2 • User Confirms

Salesperson, buyer or accountant clicks Confirm.

STEP 3 • Auto-Hold

Matching documents pause and Tier 1 gets a To-Do activity.

STEP 4 • Tier Decision

Approve to advance, or reject with a required written reason.

STEP 5 • Auto-Confirm

Final approval confirms the document with a full audit log.

COMPLETE VISUAL PRODUCT TOUR

Step-by-Step Screen Walkthrough

Every screen below ships out of the box with dynamic_approval_governance.

SCREEN 1 OF 6

1. Approval Categories Dashboard

Go to Smart Approvals → Configuration → Approval Categories. Ready-made templates for Business Travel, Expense Reimbursement, CapEx & Procurement, Contract Sign-Off and Special Payment Release. Set each field (Date, Amount, Quantity, Partner, Location, Reference, Product Lines) to Required, Optional or Hidden.

SCREEN 2 OF 6

2. Dynamic Multi-Tier Matrix Rules

Go to Smart Approvals → Configuration → Dynamic Matrix Rules. Pick Sales Order, Purchase Order, Customer Invoice or Vendor Bill, set a min/max amount or maximum line discount %, then build an unlimited chain of tiers (e.g. Regional Manager → Finance Director → CEO).

SCREEN 3 OF 6

3. Live Interception & Approval Matrix Tab

When a document matches a rule, confirmation is intercepted and a governance banner appears. Approve and Reject buttons show only to the active tier’s approvers, and every tier’s status is tracked in the Approval Matrix tab.

SCREEN 4 OF 6

4. Mandatory Rejection Reason Wizard

No silent rejections. A popup enforces a written reason, marks the tier as Rejected, shows a red alert banner and logs everything in the chatter.

SCREEN 5 OF 6

5. Standalone Internal Approval Request

Employees submit requests with product lines, quantities, partner links and attachments. Sequential or parallel approvers get automatic To-Do activities and sign off in one click.

SCREEN 6 OF 6

6. Official PDF Approval Certificate

An auditor-ready certificate with reference, requester, category, totals, itemized lines and a timestamped sign-off table.
