UNDERSTAND THE FULL WORKFLOW AT A GLANCE

How the End-to-End Approval Flow Works

Whether a user is confirming a high-value Sales/Purchase Order, posting a Vendor Bill, or submitting an internal CapEx/Travel request, the governance engine follows a clear 5-stage lifecycle:

1
Define Matrix Rule

Admin sets Min/Max Amount or Discount % rules & assigns Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3 approvers.

2
User Clicks Confirm

Salesperson, Buyer, or Accountant clicks Confirm on SO, PO, Invoice, or Bill.

3
Auto-Hold & Notify

If thresholds match, confirmation pauses automatically, creates approval steps & assigns To-Do activities to Tier 1.

4
Tier-by-Tier Decision

Tier 1 clicks Approve (advances to Tier 2) or Reject (opens Mandatory Reason Wizard).

5
Auto-Confirm & PDF

When the final tier approves, the Order/Invoice confirms automatically with a full audit log.

COMPLETE VISUAL PRODUCT TOUR

Step-by-Step Screen Walkthrough

Every screen below is included out of the box when you install dynamic_approval_governance in Odoo 19 Community Edition.

SCREEN 1 OF 6

1. Standalone Approval Categories Dashboard

Go to Smart Approvals → Configuration → Approval Categories. Out of the box, you get pre-configured templates for Business Travel, Expense Reimbursement, CapEx & Procurement, Contract Sign-Off, and Special Payment Release. For each category, configure whether Date, Date Period, Amount, Quantity, Contact/Partner, Location, Reference, or Product Lines are Required, Optional, or Hidden.

SCREEN 2 OF 6

2. Dynamic Multi-Tier Matrix Rules for Sales, Purchase & Accounting

Go to Smart Approvals → Configuration → Dynamic Matrix Rules. Select the target model (Sales Order, Purchase Order, Customer Invoice, or Vendor Bill), define your Minimum / Maximum Amount or Maximum Line Discount % trigger, and build an unlimited chain of sequential approval tiers (e.g., Tier 1: Regional Manager → Tier 2: Finance Director → Tier 3: CEO).

SCREEN 3 OF 6

3. Real-Time Order & Invoice Interception with Live Approval Matrix Tab

When a user clicks Confirm on a Sales Order, Purchase Order, Invoice, or Bill that matches a rule, the module intercepts confirmation, displays a clear governance alert banner, reveals the Approve & Reject Approval buttons only to the active tier's authorized approvers, and tracks every tier's status in the Approval Matrix tab.

SCREEN 4 OF 6

4. Mandatory Rejection Reason Wizard & Chatter Audit Trail

Approvers can never reject a request or order silently. Clicking Reject Approval opens a dedicated popup wizard that enforces a clear written reason, updates the tier status to Rejected, displays a red alert banner on the document, and posts the rejection details in the document's chatter.

SCREEN 5 OF 6

5. Standalone Internal Approval Request Form (`APR/2026/0001`)

Employees submit internal requests with itemized product lines, quantities, partner links, and supporting documents. Sequential or parallel approvers receive automated Odoo To-Do activities and sign off with a single click.

SCREEN 6 OF 6

6. Printable Official PDF Approval Certificate

Generate a clean, auditor-ready PDF Approval Certificate showing the request reference, requester, category, financial totals, itemized lines, and the complete timestamped digital sign-off table.
