7-Step Operational Master Playbook

How global supply chains achieve 100% landed cost accuracy in under 2 minutes.

1

Incoming Receipt Validation

Warehouse teams validate customs container receipts in Odoo Inventory as normal (`stock.picking`).

2

Create Landed Cost Voucher

Finance or logistics opens Inventory > Operations > Landed Costs & Duties and creates a new voucher.

3

Select Target Receipts & Multi-Pickings

Select one or multiple transfer pickings to pool all arriving stock lines for cross-allocation.

4

Enter Duty & Freight Cost Lines

Add Sea Freight, Customs Tariffs, Terminal Handling, and Brokerage fees in any currency with specific split methods.

5

One-Click Mathematical Allocation

Click Compute Landed Cost to instantly populate line-by-line initial costs, additional landed costs, and final unit values.

6

Post to General Ledger & Cost Price

Click Post Landed Cost. The module auto-revalues product inventory valuations and records accounting entries seamlessly.

7

Print Audit Voucher & Archive

Print official PDF audit vouchers for tax authorities, customs compliance, and executive financial auditing.
